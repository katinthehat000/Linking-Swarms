Inputs are in the following order, with variable names from simulations also included:
-Preferred curvature, prefK01
-Timesteps to fold into preferred curvature, Nbend (typically 0 (fast coiling)  or 25000 (slow coiling))
-Radius, h
-Young's modulus, E
-Density, rho
-Viscous damping parameter, eta
-Friction damping parameter, zeta
-Skin drag, skindrag
-Random force strength, driftForce
-Filament repulsion strength, Kbulk
-Boundary force scale, bdryForceStrength
-inverse boundary friction, fStrength



The above default values correspond to (in order):


prefK01 = 1.2; %target curvature
Nbend = 0; %timesteps to fold into preferred curvature, typically 0 or 25000
h = 0.3; %radius
Eb = 10^5;  %bending modulus
rho = 0.2; %density
eta = 100; %viscous damping
zeta = 120; %friction damping
skindrag = 8.5; %drag
driftForce = 65; %Magnitude of random force
Kbulk = 200; %sets ribbon-ribbon contact forces
bdryForceStrength = 100; %sets force from boundary
fStrength = 2; %inverse friction parameter, appears in boundary contact force
